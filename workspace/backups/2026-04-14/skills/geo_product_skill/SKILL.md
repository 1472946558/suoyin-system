# SKILL.md - GEO Product

## Purpose

All knowledge, strategy, and context for the GEO (AI Search) product. Any agent working on GEO must read this first.

## Product Vision

Build an AI-powered search workflow product that helps businesses optimize their search visibility in the age of AI search engines.

## Product Hypothesis

- Traditional SEO is evolving into GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- Businesses need tools to monitor and optimize their AI search presence
- Content generation tailored for AI search engines is underserved
- Monitoring + generation + optimization = complete workflow

## Target Users

- Marketing teams at mid-market companies
- SEO agencies expanding to AI search
- Content teams needing AI search visibility
- Business owners wanting to track AI search mentions
